# Plan 12.1: Wire `handleOpenFolder` and Batch Execution CTA in Studio Hooks

## Objective
Fix the functional regressions identified during the milestone audit:
1. Pass `handleOpenFolder` through `useStudioContainerSetup`, `StudioCanvas`, `StudioPreviewSection`, into `<DropZone />` (`onAddBatch={handleOpenFolder}`).
2. Re-order hook invocation in `useStudioContainerSetup` so `useBatchSetup` is created before `useStudioActions`, enabling `batchItems` and `handleStartBatchUpscale` to be passed into `useStudioActions`.
3. In `useStudioActions.handleStartUpscale`, call `handleStartBatchUpscale()` when `batchItems.length > 1`.
